Engineering focus

What I bring, in any industry

Games are one of the most demanding real-time environments there are: a hard 16 ms frame budget, physics and AI running in parallel, and the same code shipping on several hardware platforms. These are the skills that carry over directly.

01Concurrency & synchronisation

Asynchronous computations with mutex-based synchronisation and multithreaded AI workloads, designed to run alongside a real-time main loop without stalls or data races.

02Performance engineering

CPU/GPU profiling and frame-budget analysis with platform profilers, finding and removing bottlenecks in production code under strict timing constraints.

03Physics & vehicle simulation

Custom collision callbacks in the Chaos physics engine, vehicle-interaction logic and close work with R&D on vehicle control and input handling.

04AI & ML-driven control

Contributed to a neural-network vehicle AI and its training process with designers, alongside classic control loops, path following and behaviour systems.

05Debugging large codebases

Crash investigation in physics and level-streaming code, bug triage and fixes in a large, long-lived C++ codebase shared by many teams.

06Cross-platform delivery

Platform-specific debugging on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch, with internal tooling built to speed up testing and iteration.
